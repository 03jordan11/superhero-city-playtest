**🦸 Playtest — Useful Commands**

**Open the developer console**
Press the backtick key (usually below Esc).
Type a command and press Enter. Press Esc to close and resume playing.

**⚡ Get powered up**
• `add pp 50` — Adds 50 power points.
• `add xp 100000` — Adds 100,000 XP to level up.
• Press P after closing the console to spend your points.

**💾 Save & load**
• `save` — Saves your character’s progression.
• `load` — Restores your saved progression.

Run `load` when starting a new session to continue your progress. Saves don’t include your position or spawned encounters.

**🚨 Spawn encounters**
• `spawn gang_activity` — Gang fight scaled to your level.
• `spawn pirates` — Fight pirates aboard a cargo ship.
• `spawn ship_docking` — Ship docking challenge.
• `spawn helicopter_chase` — Fight an armed helicopter.
• `spawn rescue` — Rescue an injured civilian and take them to the hospital.

Close the console after spawning an encounter, then follow the waypoint.

**🔧 Extra help**
• `help` — Lists available commands.
• `status` — Shows your stats and points.
